Raising the injection rate from 2.2 % to 10.3 % raises the ROC-AUC of every score, and the ordering of methods does not change within the seed spread: reconstruction with a GAT and with a GCN are indistinguishable, and the attention-only and combined scores stay a few hundredths higher. With more injected structural cliques and swapped attributes, anomalies become easier to separate, so absolute numbers on injected data should not be read as expected performance on organic anomalies, where the benchmark paper reports much lower scores for deep detectors [@liu2022bond]. Sensitivity to the cascade probability is shown in the influence figures of Section 5.2: the ranking quality of degree, PageRank and the learned heads changes with p, and relative to the non-learned uniform-attention control, received attention is identical on Cora and lower on Weibo at every p we tried. Degree has the highest correlation at the smallest p, while the GCN head improves as p grows.
